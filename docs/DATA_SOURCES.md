# Catalogue Sources And Limits

## Verification Date

This catalogue contains **33 established pujas**: North 7, Central 3, South 15,
Behala 4, Salt Lake 3 and New Town 1. Bengali names and concise descriptions are
original bilingual editorial copy; alternate Latin spellings aid name matching.

**`verifiedOn` means the public source page was checked, NOT confirmation of a
2026 event, opening, theme, timetable, gate, safety arrangement or accessibility.**
The requested reference date was 13 October 2026. These fetches actually occurred
on **14 October 2026**, as confirmed by the system clock and the official Metro
page. Consequently `verifiedOn` and `SOURCE_CHECKED_ON` are `2026-10-14`; there is
no evidence of a 13 October check to backdate. This is a deliberate provenance
correction, not an assertion of newer event confirmation.

All 33 entries have at least one accessible listing source. There are **four
distinct primary listing URLs**, plus the two corroborating directory pages
below. These are public third-party listings, not independent organizer
confirmations. No firsthand visit, current queue, live crowd, official ranking,
current-year theme, opening date, security provision or step-free access has
been verified or encoded. Source claims about those subjects were not imported.

## Metro Provenance

The authoritative network reference is the [Metro Railway system-map
page](https://mtp.indianrailways.gov.in/view_section.jsp?lang=0&id=0,1,285), labelled
"SYSTEM MAP UPDATED ON 31ST MARCH 2026", last reviewed 20 August 2026. Its
[published map image](https://mtp.indianrailways.gov.in/uploads/images/1787227418006-SYSTEM%20MAP%20OF%20METRO%20RAILWAY%20CORRECTED%20UPTO%20310326%20APPROVED.jpeg)
is linked, not redistributed as a licensed application image. The
[official notices homepage](https://mtp.indianrailways.gov.in/index.jsp?lang=0)
is the place to check changing services. A page-check date is not a new-line
opening date or a service guarantee. No special-night timetable is encoded.

The [public station inventory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kolkata_Metro_stations)
describes the 12-station Green Line as operational as of 22 August 2025, including
the Sealdah-Esplanade connection. This is historical network evidence, not live
availability. The dataset retains the nine consecutive stops from Sector V to
Esplanade and all 15 consecutive Blue stops from Shyambazar to Mahanayak Uttam
Kumar. It omits Howrah, northern/southern Blue extensions and other lines rather
than inventing service on them. No Orange Line station or New Town Metro service
is modeled. Behala entries do not pretend Purple Line stops are Blue/Green stops.

Station coordinates are approximate **station-area anchors** from these public
Wikipedia coordinate records and their GeoHack/map references, not official
survey coordinates. The MediaWiki public `prop=coordinates` response was checked
for the unambiguous pages; the four `(Kolkata)` pages were read directly after
their unqualified titles resolved ambiguously. No platform, exit or accessibility
information is imported.

`blue-esplanade` and `green-esplanade` intentionally share the general station
coordinate but remain different IDs. Both names are exactly `Esplanade`, because
the existing graph recognizes that name to add its estimated interchange walk.
Shared pins do not mean zero transfer time, a shared platform or a verified
transfer passage. `order` is a consecutive position **within each modeled line**,
not a station code. Skipping an intermediate station would incorrectly shorten
the graph's stop overhead, so the validator checks contiguous order.

`nearestMetro`, when present, is a station-ID planning hint within this limited
network, not a measured accessible walking route or a guarantee of the closest
exit. Missing values are intentional for places outside the modeled corridors
or with particularly uncertain locality pins. Do not infer no Metro exists there.

## Starts, Routes And Estimates

Eight manually selectable starts are provided: Esplanade, Shyambazar, Kalighat,
Girish Park, Rabindra Sarobar, Sector V and Karunamoyee station areas, plus the
New Town Sarbojanin locality. Station-area starts inherit the sourced coordinates
above; the New Town start reuses its K locality reference. None represents the
user's device position or an actual GPS fix. None specifies an entrance number.

Four original compact examples use those landmarks: Bagbazar to Ahiritola,
Central Kolkata squares, Deshapriya/Ballygunge and Kalighat/Chetla. They are
geographic suggestions, not booked tours or proven walkable street itineraries.
The validator limits each straight-line leg to under 2 km and each complete
example to under 5 km; those bounds are **not street-walking distances**.

**Route availability warning:** annual venue changes, barricades, one-way crowd
flows, road closures, private-house entry restrictions and Metro closures can
invalidate a route. Check the organizer and official transport/police notices
on the day. In particular, the graph can estimate Metro at an hour when there is
no service; always check the last train and return journey. No curated route
promises safe passage, accessibility, opening hours or completion time.

`visitMinutes` (25-45) is an editorial dwell allowance excluding queues and
transport, not a source-measured wait or current capacity estimate. `popularity`
is a static editorial planning weight: 3 for the headline major selection, 2 for
other established stops. It is not attendance, a live report or an official
ranking. `featured` is editorial only. The application must keep estimated crowd
model output separate from visitor reports and verified observations.

## Image And Attribution

The optional local image is [public/images/puja-kolkata.webp](../public/images/puja-kolkata.webp).
Its source, original URL, author, historical date, license and conversion details
are recorded in [public/images/puja-kolkata.LICENSE.md](../public/images/puja-kolkata.LICENSE.md).
The source identifies Kolkata Durga Puja in **2024**, not a specific catalogue
pandal. Never caption it as the current year's theme or a named pandal. Display
the author and linked CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution with the image; do not imply
endorsement. The 5.47 MB original is not downloaded; only a bounded Commons
thumbnail is used for the small WebP derivative.

Public listing facts were selected manually and descriptions written afresh;
third-party prose, photos, crowd reports and route descriptions were not copied.
Linked maps retain their providers' terms. OpenStreetMap-based map rendering must
retain visible [OpenStreetMap contributor attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
and applicable tile-provider attribution; this catalogue does not replace those
obligations. Wikipedia structured facts are linked above; no article prose or
official Metro artwork is redistributed.

## Exports And Checks

[src/data/pandals.ts](../src/data/pandals.ts) exports `PANDALS: Pandal[]`,
`METRO_STATIONS: MetroStation[]` (re-exported), `START_POINTS: Landmark[]`,
`CURATED_ROUTES: CuratedRoute[]`, `SOURCE_CHECKED_ON` and `CATALOGUE_NOTICE`.
Each `CuratedRoute` has `id`, `name`, `nameBn`, `description`, `descriptionBn`,
`stopIds: string[]` and `startId: string`.
[src/data/metro.ts](../src/data/metro.ts) also exports `METRO_NETWORK_URL`,
`METRO_NOTICES_URL` and the bilingual `METRO_NOTICE`.

Run `npm run test:unit -- tests/unit/catalogue.test.ts` from the project directory.
The six focused tests check ID uniqueness, 30-40 entries, all six zones,
coordinate bounds, Bengali fields, numeric planning values, source/approximation
labels, station references, contiguous per-line order, the real catalogue's
Esplanade graph interchange and compact routes with manual starts. They validate
internal integrity, not the truth of annual events or field accessibility.

Verification on 14 October 2026: all six catalogue tests passed; the current
complete unit suite passed **72 tests in six files**. A strict, no-output
TypeScript check of the two data modules and catalogue test also passed. A
separate source-register check matched every documented pandal coordinate to its
exported value (33/33). The 720 x 960 WebP rendered successfully and is 114,146
bytes; its source thumbnail was 364,322 bytes. No UI or root configuration was
edited; the catalogue test uses the specifically permitted existing unit-test
directory. No commit or deployment was performed.
