retains the license listed below. Map-data rights are separate from photograph
rights. These are historical photographs, not evidence of any 2026 decoration,
theme, opening, queue or entrance.

changes, and the identity-review reason. Capture dates are source assertions;
their time zone is not inferred. Metadata was checked on 17 October 2026, between
10:25:49.397Z and 10:28:52.353Z. Download times are separately recorded.

### Reuse And Attribution

Keep the original title, named photographer, working source and exact license
links, and the change notice with redistributions. Display concise attribution
with the image and link to this record for full details. Do not imply that a
photographer, Wikimedia Commons or a puja committee endorses PujaPath.

For example, the new portrait's credit is:

> Durga Face - Kolkata 2015-10-22 6595.JPG, Biswarup Ganguly, via Wikimedia
> Commons, CC BY 3.0. Resized and converted to WebP; full frame retained.

CC BY-SA derivatives remain under their respective **CC BY-SA 3.0** or
**CC BY-SA 4.0** licenses. Do not replace either with the other or apply the map's
database license to the photographs. CC0 credit is retained for traceability
although the dedication does not require attribution.

photographs, stock city maps or downloaded map tiles. The complete file list,
pin coordinates, bounds, source-edge counts, sizes and SHA-256 values are in
[pandals/area-provenance.json](pandals/area-provenance.json).

mixed-media directory. Retain the visible OpenStreetMap attribution, provide a
working copyright link with the displayed map, and retain the database notice
